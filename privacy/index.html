Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information Furthew Entertainment ("we", "us") collects when you visit furthew.com, join our email list or send us a bug report, and what we do with it. Furthew Entertainment is run by Matt Parker in Gilmer, Texas.

What we collect

  • Email list. When you sign up on our subscribe page, we collect your email address. Signups are handled by MailerLite, our email service. You confirm your address before we send you anything. MailerLite may record whether you open our emails and which links you click.
  • Bug reports. When you use our bug report form, we collect what you type (your name if you give it, your email address and your description of the problem), plus the game version, the screen you came from and basic device and browser details. Reports are delivered to us by email through FormSubmit.
  • Meta Pixel. Our pages use the Meta Pixel, a small piece of code from Meta (the company behind Facebook and Instagram). It uses cookies and similar technology to tell Meta which of our pages you visit and when you join our email list. Meta may connect this with your Facebook or Instagram account and use it to measure and show ads. Meta handles this data under its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting. Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical information such as your IP address to keep the service running and secure.

How we use it

  • To send you news about Ad Valorem, our Kickstarter campaign and our other games and apps, if you signed up.
  • To read, fix and reply to bug reports.
  • To understand how people find our site and to measure our Facebook and Instagram ads.

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with the services named above, which process it for us, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe any time with the link at the bottom of every email.
  • Ad tracking: you can manage how Meta uses this information in your Facebook ad preferences, and you can block or clear cookies in your browser settings.
  • See or delete your information: email us and we'll tell you what we have and delete it on request.

Other sites

Our site links to Kickstarter, Facebook, TikTok and the Ad Valorem Pocket web game. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and this policy doesn't cover them.

Children

Our site isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
